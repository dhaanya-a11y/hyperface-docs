# Offer Scope

## What is Offer Scope?

**Offer Scope** captures the core identifying details of an offer and defines where the offer applies — which issuer, program type, and programs can use it. It is configured after selecting the **Outcome Type** and before defining the **Offer User Base**.

<Note>
Offer Scope defines the commercial and organizational boundaries of an offer — its identity and where it is applicable. It does not define who is eligible for the offer; that is configured separately in **Offer User Base**.
</Note>

## Basic Offer Details

Provide the core details that identify the offer and control how long it runs.

| Field | What It Represents | Example |
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| **Offer Name** | A unique, identifiable name for the offer, shown in the Hyperface Dashboard. | Fuel Cashback July 2026 |
| **Offer Description** | A brief description of the offer's purpose, for internal reference in the dashboard. | 5% cashback on eligible fuel transactions |
| **Offer Duration** | The start and end date and time during which the offer remains active. Transactions outside this window are not evaluated for the offer. | 01 Jul 2026, 00:00 – 31 Jul 2026, 23:59 |

### Eligibility Level

**Eligibility Level** determines whether offer eligibility is evaluated per account or across all accounts belonging to a customer.

| Consideration | Account Level | Customer Level |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility evaluated for | Each account independently | All accounts belonging to the customer |
| Best for | Offers tied to a specific account or card product | Offers meant to reward the overall customer relationship, regardless of which account is used |

<Note>
Changing the Eligibility Level changes what the engine treats as a single eligible unit. Choose **Account Level** when the offer should apply separately to each account a customer holds, and **Customer Level** when activity across all of a customer's accounts should count towards the same offer.
</Note>

**Example: Rewarding a customer's overall relationship**

To run a cashback offer that rewards a customer regardless of which of their accounts is used:
1. Set **Eligibility Level** to **Customer Level**.
2. Continue with the remaining setup.

Transactions made from any account belonging to the customer are considered together for this offer.

## Offer Applicability

Define where the offer is available.

| Field | What It Represents | Example |
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| **Issuer** | The issuer for which the offer will be available. | HDFC Bank |
| **Program Type** | The type of program the offer applies to. | Credit Card |
| **Apply to all current and future programs** | When enabled, the offer applies to every existing program of the selected type, and automatically extends to any new program created later. | Enabled |
| **Program** | The specific program(s) the offer applies to. Only shown when **Apply to all current and future programs** is disabled. | Platinum Credit Card |

![Step 4 - Configure Offer Applicability](/images/f.png)

<Note>
**Program** is only required when **Apply to all current and future programs** is disabled. If it is enabled, the offer is not restricted to specific programs and does not need this field configured.
</Note>

**Example: Restricting an offer to one program**

To launch a cashback offer only for the Platinum Credit Card program:
1. Select the applicable **Issuer** and **Program Type**.
2. Leave **Apply to all current and future programs** disabled.
3. Select **Platinum Credit Card** under **Program**.

Only transactions made under the Platinum Credit Card program are considered for this offer, even if the customer holds other programs under the same issuer.

## What Happens After Configuration?

Offer Scope establishes the offer's identity, active duration, eligibility level, and the issuer and programs it applies to. Once configured, these details are used as the boundary within which the **Offer User Base** and **Transaction Eligibility** are evaluated — a transaction is only considered for the offer if it falls within the configured Offer Duration and belongs to an applicable Issuer and Program.

## Complete the Configuration

After configuring the Offer Scope:

1. Review the configured **Offer Name**, **Offer Description**, **Offer Duration**, and **Eligibility Level**.
2. Confirm the configured **Offer Applicability** — Issuer, Program Type, and Program.
3. Click **Continue** to proceed to the **Offer User Base** configuration.
