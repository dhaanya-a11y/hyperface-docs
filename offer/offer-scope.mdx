## Overview
The configured scope establishes the offer's applicability before proceeding to configure the **Offer User Base**.

During this step, configure the **Issuer**, **Program Type**, and **Programs** for which the offer should be available. The selected scope establishes the offer's applicability before proceeding to configure the **Offer User Base**.

<Note>

**Offer Scope** defines **where** an offer is applicable. It does **not** determine **who** is eligible to receive the offer. Customer eligibility is configured separately in **Offer User Base**.

</Note>

---

## Basic Offer Details

Configure the basic information that identifies the offer and defines its active duration.

| **Field**|**Description**|**Example**|
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| **Offer Name** | A unique name used to identify the offer within the Hyperface Dashboard.| Fuel Cashback July 2026 |
| **Offer Description** |A brief description used to identify the purpose of the offer.| 5% cashback on eligible fuel transactions |
| **Offer Duration** |Defines the start and end date during which the offer remains active. Only transactions that occur within the configured duration are evaluated for the offer. | 01 Jul 2026, 00:00 – 31 Jul 2026, 23:59 |

### Eligibility Level

The **Eligibility Level** determines whether offer eligibility is evaluated at the **Account** level or the **Customer** level.

| Consideration | Account Level | Customer Level |
|---|---|---|
| Eligibility evaluated for | Each account independently | All accounts belonging to the customer |
| Recommended Use| Offers tied to a specific account or card | Offers meant to reward the overall customer relationship, regardless of which account is used |

<Tip>

Choose the **Eligibility Level** based on how the offer should evaluate customer activity.

- **Account Level** evaluates each account independently.
- **Customer Level** evaluates activity across all accounts associated with the customer.

</Tip>

**Example: Rewarding a customer's overall relationship**

To run a cashback offer that rewards customers regardless of which of their accounts is used:

1. Set **Eligibility Level** to **Customer Level**.
2. Continue with the remaining setup.

Transactions made from any account belonging to the customer are considered together for this offer.

<Tip>

**Not sure which to pick?**

- Choose **Account Level** if the offer is tied to a specific product, such as a particular card.
- Choose **Customer Level** if the offer should reward the customer's overall relationship, regardless of which account or product is used.

</Tip>

## Offer Applicability

Configure the issuer and program for which the offer is applicable.

| Field | What It Represents | Example |
|-------|---------------------|---------|
| **Issuer** |Select the issuer for which the offer is created.| HDFC Bank |
| **Program Type** | Select the program type to which the offer applies.| Credit Card |
| **Apply to all current and future programs** | Makes the offer applicable to all existing programs of the selected Program Type and automatically includes any programs created in the future. | Enabled |
| **Program** | Select the specific program(s) to which the offer applies. This field is displayed only when **Apply to all current and future programs** is disabled. | Platinum Credit Card |

<Warning>

Changing the configured **Issuer**, **Program Type**, or **Program** after completing later configuration steps may require those configurations to be reviewed.

</Warning>

**Example: Restricting an offer to one program**

To launch a cashback offer only for the **Platinum Credit Card** program:

1. Select the applicable **Issuer** and **Program Type**.
2. Leave **Apply to all current and future programs** disabled.
3. Select **Platinum Credit Card** under **Program**.

The offer is applicable only to transactions associated with the selected program.
---

## What Happens After Configuration?

Once the **Offer Scope** is configured, the offer is associated with the selected **Issuer**, **Program Type**, and **Program**. These settings define where the offer is applicable and provide the context for the remaining offer configuration steps.

Only transactions associated with the configured **Issuer**, **Program**, and **Offer Duration** are considered during offer evaluation.

---

## Complete the Configuration

After configuring the **Offer Scope**:

1. Review the configured **Offer Name**, **Offer Description**, and **Offer Duration**.
2. Verify the selected **Eligibility Level**.
3. Confirm the configured **Issuer**, **Program Type**, and **Program**.
4. Click **Continue** to proceed to **Offer User Base**.
