## Overview

The **Offer Type** determines how customers become eligible to receive an offer benefit and is the first configuration selected during the offer creation journey.

Each Offer Type represents a different reward model and defines how customer eligibility is evaluated throughout the offer lifecycle. The selected Offer Type also determines the configuration steps, eligibility criteria, and options available during the remaining stages of offer creation.

Hyperface supports five Offer Types: **Transactional**, **Milestone**, **Merchant**, **Gamified**, and **Display Only**.

<Warning>

The selected **Offer Type** determines the configuration flow for the remainder of the offer creation journey. Changing the Offer Type after configuring subsequent steps may require those configurations to be recreated.

</Warning>

## Comparing Offer Types

| **Consideration** | **Transactional** | **Milestone** | **Merchant** | **Gamified** | **Display Only** |
|-------------------|-------------------|---------------|--------------|--------------|------------------|
| **Reward Trigger by** | A single qualifying transaction | Achieving a predefined spend or transaction milestone | An eligible purchase at a participating merchant | Completing configured challenges or progressing through levels | Not applicable |
| **Eligibility Evaluation** | Each qualifying transaction is evaluated independently | Customer progress is tracked until the configured milestone is achieved | Eligibility is evaluated using merchant-specific criteria | Customer progress is tracked across configured levels or activities | No reward evaluation |
| **Recommended Use Cases** | Cashback or transaction-based reward campaigns | Campaigns that encourage cumulative spending or transaction activity | Merchant-funded or partner-specific reward campaigns | Interactive and engagement-driven reward campaigns | Displaying promotional or informational offers for customer visibility |

<Note>
The eligibility configuration shown later in the offer creation journey depends on the selected **Offer Type**.
</Note>

## Configuration Flow

The configuration flow differs based on the selected **Offer Type**. Depending on the selected offer, different eligibility configuration steps are presented during the offer creation journey.

| **Offer Type** | **Eligibility Step Shown Later** |
|----------------|----------------------------------|
| **Transactional** | **Transaction Eligibility** – Configure conditions based on individual transaction attributes. |
| **Milestone** | **Milestone Eligibility** – Configure milestone conditions based on cumulative spend or transaction thresholds. |
| **Merchant** | No separate eligibility screen – Eligibility is determined through the selected merchant and the configured **Offer User Base**. |
| **Gamified** | **Gamification Levels** – Configure levels, milestones, and challenges that customers must complete to earn rewards. |
| **Display Only** | No eligibility configuration – This offer is presented for visibility purposes only. |
---

## Transactional Offer

Rewards customers when an individual transaction satisfies the configured eligibility criteria. Each qualifying transaction is evaluated independently, making this offer type suitable for transaction-based reward campaigns such as cashback and reward points.

## Display Only Offer

Intended to present promotional or informational content to customers for visibility purposes only.
This offer type is used when the objective is to display offer information without configuring a reward journey.

<div className="flex justify-center my-6">
  <img
    src="/images/v.20.png"
    alt="Display Only Offer"
    className="w-[700px] rounded-2xl border border-gray-200 shadow-lg"
  />
</div>

**Key characteristics**

- Displays offer information for visibility purposes.
- Appears as a **Display Only** offer during offer creation.
- Does not introduce a reward-based configuration journey.

<Note>

The product interface identifies this offer as **"Presented for visibility purpose only."** Any additional reward or eligibility behavior depends on subsequent product configuration and is not defined at the Offer Type selection step.

</Note>

## Example: Choosing the Correct Offer Type

A bank wants to reward customers who spend a total of **₹50,000** across transactions in a calendar month, rather than rewarding any single transaction.

1. Select **Milestone Offer**, since the reward depends on cumulative spend rather than one qualifying transaction.
2. Continue with the remaining setup.

Selecting a **Transactional Offer** would instead evaluate each qualifying transaction independently rather than tracking cumulative spend towards the configured target.

## Which Offer Type Should You Select?

<Tip>
- Reward every qualifying transaction independently → **Transactional Offer**
- Reward customers after achieving cumulative spend or transaction milestones → **Milestone Offer**
- Reward purchases made at participating merchants → **Merchant Offer**
- Reward customers for completing challenges or activities → **Gamified Offer**
- Display promotional or informational content for customer visibility → **Display Only Offer**

</Tip>
