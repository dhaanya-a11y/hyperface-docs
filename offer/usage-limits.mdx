## Overview

**Usage Limits** control how often an individual customer can earn rewards through an offer across cycles. All limit types are optional—configure only the limits relevant to your campaign design.

## Why Usage Limits Matter

At enterprise scale, an offer is not evaluated against a single customer. Instead, it runs across an entire portfolio, often covering hundreds of thousands of accounts throughout the campaign duration.

Without a per-customer cap, a small number of high-frequency or high-value customers can consume a disproportionate share of the reward spend, leaving comparatively less benefit for the remaining eligible customers.

Usage Limits help make reward distribution predictable and manageable by:

- **Cost Containment** – Limits how much a single customer can receive from an offer, helping keep campaign spend proportional to the intended reach.
- **Fair Distribution** – Promotes a consistent reward experience across the eligible customer base instead of rewarding transaction frequency alone.
- **Abuse Resistance** – Reduces the possibility of customers repeatedly generating qualifying transactions solely to maximize rewards.
- **Predictable Reporting** – Provides campaign owners and finance teams with a per-customer reward ceiling, making reward expenditure easier to forecast.

<Note>

**Usage Limits** and **Budget** control reward exposure from different perspectives.

- **Budget** limits the total reward spend across **all customers** participating in the offer.
- **Usage Limits** limit the rewards an **individual customer** can earn within a specified duration.

While **Budget** determines the total reward allocation for an offer, **Usage Limits** determine the maximum rewards an individual customer can receive during the selected duration.

</Note>

## Usage Limit Configuration

Configure the following fields while defining a usage limit.

| **Field** | **Description** | **Example** |
|-----------|-----------------|-------------|
| **Duration** | Select the period during which the usage limit should be evaluated. | Monthly |
| **Limit Type** | Select the type of limit to enforce for the offer. | Cashback Amount |
| **Limit Value** | Specify the maximum reward amount or usage value a customer can receive during the selected duration. | ₹500 |

---

## Duration

The **Duration** specifies the period during which the configured usage limit is considered.

## Limit Type

The **Limit Type** specifies the type of usage limit to be applied.

The following limit types are available:

| **Limit Type** | **Description** |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **Both** | Applies both available limit types. |
| **Count** | Applies the configured count-based limit. |
| **Reward Points** | Applies the configured reward points limit. |

---

## Limit Value

The **Limit Value** specifies the maximum value permitted for the selected **Limit Type** within the configured **Duration**.

**Example**

If **Duration** is **Month**, **Limit Type** is **Reward Points**, and **Limit Value** is **5,000**, a customer can continue earning reward points until the configured monthly limit is reached.

---

## Multiple Usage Limits

If the campaign requires more than one usage limit:

1. Click **Add More**.
2. Configure the additional usage limit.
3. Repeat the process until all required limits have been added.

Multiple usage limits can be configured for the same offer, allowing different limits to be applied across different durations.

<Tip>

For example, a campaign can include both a monthly usage limit and an annual usage limit to manage rewards over different time periods.

</Tip>

---

## Example

### Mass-Market Cashback Card

A bank runs a **5% fuel cashback** campaign across its debit card portfolio.

Configure the following:

- **Duration:** Monthly
- **Limit Type:** Cashback Amount
- **Limit Value:** ₹300

With this configuration, no customer can receive more than **₹300 cashback** during a month, regardless of the number of qualifying fuel transactions.

---

## Complete the Configuration

After configuring the usage limits:

1. Review all configured usage limits.
2. Verify that the selected **Duration**, **Limit Type**, and **Limit Value** align with the campaign requirements.
3. Confirm how the configured usage limits interact with the offer's overall **Budget**.
4. Click **Continue** to proceed to the **Posting** configuration.
